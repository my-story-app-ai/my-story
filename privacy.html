Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

My Story is an early-stage digital product that helps users create personalized illustrated stories from photos and memories they choose to provide.

Information we may process

Depending on the product version, users may provide email addresses, story prompts, uploaded photos and other information needed to create a personalized story.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the requested digital product, support the user, maintain security and improve the service.

Photos and personal content

Users should upload only content they have the right and permission to use. We do not claim ownership of user photos or personal memories.

Payments

Payments may be processed by a third-party merchant-of-record/payment provider. My Story does not store full card details.
