Privacy Policy

My Story is an early-stage digital product that helps users create personalized illustrated snapshots and stories from photos and memories they choose to provide.

Information we may process

Depending on the product version, users may provide uploaded photos, story prompts, names, optional context details and other information needed to create the requested personalized digital product.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the requested digital product, maintain security and improve the service.

Photos and personal content

Users should upload only content they have the right and permission to use. We do not claim ownership of user photos or personal memories.

Storage and retention

The product is being designed to minimize storage of uploaded personal content. Specific retention periods will be published before commercial launch.

Payments

Payments may be processed by a third-party merchant-of-record or payment provider. My Story does not store full card details.
