# My Story

**Turn your photos and memories into personalized illustrated stories.**

My Story is an early-stage digital product for creating personalized illustrated stories from user-provided photos, memories and story ideas.

## MVP concept

1. User uploads a small set of reference photos.
2. User chooses a story type and provides a short description.
3. The app generates a personalized illustrated story.
4. The finished story is delivered digitally.

## Positioning

**Your memories. Reimagined.**

My Story is not intended to be another avatar or single-image generator. The product is focused on complete personalized narratives for moments such as birthdays, anniversaries, family trips, children's adventures and gifts.

## Initial commercial model

- One-time purchase, no subscription required.
- Starter story target price: **$9.99**
- Longer book-ready format planned at **$19.99**
- International digital distribution.
- Merchant-of-record checkout planned.
