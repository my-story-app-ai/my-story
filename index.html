FAQ

A few useful answers.

What is the difference between Snapshot and My Story?

Snapshot gives you one final personalized illustration. My Story gives you a complete multi-page illustrated story as a downloadable PDF.

How many people can I add?

You can add 1–5 people. One clear reference photo per person is required, with a second optional image.

Can I add photos of the actual place or event?

Yes. You can add extra memory photos to help describe the place, atmosphere or moment.

Is there any manual design work involved?

No. The product flow is designed to be fully automated. You can change your inputs or regenerate automatically.

Can I use it as a gift?

Yes. Birthdays, anniversaries, trips and family memories are core use cases.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Both products use one-time pricing.
