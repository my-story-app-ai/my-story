FAQ

A few useful answers.

Do I need photos from the actual event?

That is the easiest path. If you do not have them, the app can reconstruct the moment from person and place references plus your description.

What is the difference between Snapshot and My Story?

Snapshot gives you one personalized illustration. My Story creates a cover and four-scene illustrated story delivered as a PDF.

Is this just a cartoon filter?

No. The goal is not only to stylize a photo, but to use your photos and words to interpret the memory and preserve how the moment felt.

Is there any manual design work involved?

No. The product flow is designed to be fully automated.

Can I use it as a gift?

Yes. Trips, birthdays, anniversaries, family memories and “remember when...” moments are natural use cases.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Both formats use one-time pricing.
