Frequently asked questions

How many photos do I need?

The planned MVP flow will ask for a small set of clear reference photos, typically 3–6 images.

Will the illustrated characters look exactly like us?

The goal is recognizable, consistent illustrated characters, but the result is a creative interpretation rather than a photographic replica.

How long does generation take?

Generation time will depend on story length and image processing. The final delivery time will be shown before purchase.

What do I receive?

A digital illustrated story. A higher-resolution book-ready PDF is planned as an extended option.

Do you keep my photos?

Production retention rules are still being finalized. The product is being designed to minimize storage and use photos only for creating the requested story.

Can I make a story as a gift?

Yes. Birthdays, anniversaries, family memories and special occasions are core use cases for My Story.
